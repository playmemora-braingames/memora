Memora Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Contact: Kaidenbudhani@gmail.com.

Who can participate?

Memora is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Players self-confirm their age; this is not independent age verification. Research data contribution is entirely optional. Declining does not prevent gameplay.

What stays on your device?

Game progress, XP, streaks, and a limited local history of gameplay attempts are saved in browser storage. You can clear these from Privacy settings. Clearing browser data does not itself delete previously uploaded research records.

What is uploaded?

After completing two levels, adults may explicitly opt in to contributing results from future attempts only. The first two levels and all earlier attempts are never uploaded. If you opt in, Memora sends a broad adult age band, game type, level, correctness, response time, game/difficulty version and time played, linked to a randomly generated participant token. This is pseudonymous data, not guaranteed anonymous. We do not request your name, birthday, email, location or medical details for research contribution.

Purpose and hosting

These records support game difficulty analysis, reliability testing and development/evaluation of adaptive difficulty systems. They are not used for diagnosis or medical decisions. Records are processed by Supabase in the United States (US East) over encrypted connections, with restricted administrative access. GitHub Pages may separately process standard website request logs under its own policies.

Retention and deletion

Research data may be retained indefinitely unless you request deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements. You may withdraw consent and request permanent deletion at any time by opening Privacy settings on the same browser and selecting “Request deletion of my uploaded data”. This sends your stored participant token to the server, deletes your consent record and linked gameplay records, and stops future uploads. Keep your browser data until deletion is confirmed. If you lose access to that browser, email Kaidenbudhani@gmail.com; identifying your records may not be possible without your token.

Security, rights and changes

We restrict research database access, validate submissions and limit requests, but no online service is risk-free. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correction, withdrawal, deletion and complaint to a privacy authority. Contact the privacy address for questions or requests. Material changes to collection will require an updated notice and, where appropriate, fresh consent.
