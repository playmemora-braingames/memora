Memora Privacy & Data Choices

Updated November 1, 2026. Memora is an educational game, not a medical assessment or treatment.

Local gameplay data

Memora saves your chosen age range, generated nickname, XP, streaks, completed levels, and recent gameplay attempts in this browser. Attempts may include the game, level, result, response time, and time played. This lets your game progress persist. We do not request names, emails, exact birthdates, or health details.

No gameplay uploads

The current website does not upload gameplay records to Memora's cloud database or an AI-training service. Cloud saving and AI-training contribution are disabled for all ages. After two successfully completed levels, Memora may show an optional AI-training preference screen. Accepting records only a local preference; it does not activate uploads. Players under 13 cannot opt in, and teen safeguards require review before collection. Any future data transmission requires a separate reviewed notice, appropriate consent, and secure infrastructure. Declining future contributions will not prevent gameplay. The first two introductory levels will not be uploaded for training even if someone later opts in.

Clearing your data

From Worlds, open Privacy settings and choose Clear local gameplay data. This clears progress and recent gameplay attempts on this browser, but leaves your age range and nickname. You can also clear site storage in browser settings. Existing records previously uploaded under the older optional cloud-saving feature are not deleted by this local-only control; contact the project owner using a verified support channel for assistance. No contact channel has yet been published.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages serves this site. As with most websites, the hosting provider may process technical request logs, such as IP addresses, independently of the game's local progress storage. We cannot promise zero network metadata.

Future changes

Any future AI-training contribution must explain the specific fields collected, storage provider, access, retention, withdrawal, deletion, and youth eligibility before any upload is enabled. A random nickname or identifier linked across sessions can be pseudonymous rather than fully anonymous.
