Memora Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026 · Applies to: Memora's public browser game and its optional, currently disabled AI-training contribution feature.

Current status: Memora does not send gameplay records to an AI-training database. Choosing “I agree” currently stores only a preference in your browser. This policy describes current behavior and separately explains a planned feature. It is not an announcement that collection has started.

1. Who runs Memora and how to contact us

Memora is an independently developed browser-based memory game. The project operator is responsible for decisions about any future AI-training data collection. A verified privacy contact and operator contact details have not yet been published. Before any remote collection is activated, this section must be completed with an accessible privacy contact and any other legally required operator information. Until then, do not send sensitive information or consent requests through unverified channels.

2. What Memora stores on your device now

When you play, Memora stores progress in your browser's local storage: experience points (XP), streaks, completed levels and a limited history of gameplay attempts. A gameplay attempt may contain a randomly generated record ID, game version, game type, level, whether the answer was correct, response time in milliseconds and a timestamp. The game also stores a count of completed rounds, whether the training choice has been shown, and, if you make a choice, an age range and an accept/decline preference. These values are used to operate the game and remember your settings.

Memora does not ask for your real name, email address, precise birthday, postal address, location, or medical information. Nicknames are no longer required. Some older visitors may have leftover data from previous versions in their browser.

3. What Memora sends now

No gameplay attempts, age range or training preference are currently uploaded by the Memora game. There is no active AI-training uploader or active cloud progress synchronization. GitHub Pages, which hosts the website, may separately receive standard web requests and technical information such as IP address, user agent and request timestamps. GitHub's handling of those requests is governed by its own policies; Memora does not control its hosting logs.

4. Optional AI-training contribution — planned, not active

After two successfully completed levels, Memora shows a choice about helping improve future adaptive difficulty. Declining does not restrict the game. Accepting currently saves only a local preference, not permission for a live transfer. No records from the first two introductory levels will be uploaded or backfilled for training.

If a reviewed collection feature is activated in the future, the proposed minimum fields are broad age band, game type, level, correct/incorrect result, response time, attempt number, difficulty or game version, and time played. A random participant token may be used to link contributions and support withdrawal. A persistent token means data can be pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. We will not request names or precise birthdays for this feature.

Planned uses are measuring difficulty, improving pacing, training and evaluating an adaptive difficulty model, testing reliability, and detecting invalid or abusive submissions. Data will not be used to diagnose health or cognitive conditions, and Memora is not a medical test.

5. Age protections and choice

Players under 13 cannot opt into training contributions. Players aged 13–17 are not eligible for remote contribution under the initial adult-only launch plan; any later youth participation requires a separate reviewed process, including any applicable guardian authorization and age-appropriate protections. Adults aged 18 or older may be eligible only after a finalized notice and secure system are launched. A self-selected age band alone is not reliable proof of age.

Consent must be informed, specific, optional and withdrawable. Refusing consent does not affect access to gameplay. The existing local “agree” setting is not being treated as authorization to begin future uploads automatically: a new affirmative decision will be required before any collection begins.

6. Proposed storage, security and access

A private Supabase PostgreSQL schema has been prepared for future consent and gameplay records. The database project is hosted in the United States (US East). No public read/write access has been granted to the new training tables. If launched, records would be transmitted over encrypted connections to a validated server endpoint; access would be restricted to authorized project administrators and service providers who need it for the stated purposes. Safeguards will include server-side consent checks, rate limits, access controls, validation, monitoring and deletion procedures. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Cross-border processing and any applicable Canadian privacy obligations must be reviewed before activation. We do not currently sell gameplay data or use it for targeted advertising.

7. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay records remain in the browser until you clear them, your browser removes them, or a storage limit replaces older records. The game currently retains at most 1,000 recent local attempts. From the Worlds screen, open Privacy settings and choose Clear local gameplay data; you can also clear the site's browser storage. Clearing local data does not erase provider access logs or any data previously uploaded by an older version of Memora.

Remote retention period: not finalized. Before uploads are enabled, Memora must publish a specific retention schedule, a contact method, and a tested process for withdrawal, access requests and deletion of related remote records. Withdrawal will stop future collection and trigger the applicable deletion process, subject to any legally required retention. Do not assume deleting browser storage alone can erase remote data.

8. Legal basis and your rights

Current on-device storage supports game operation. The planned optional training feature is intended to rely on a valid affirmative consent process where required. The applicable legal framework depends on the operator's status, users' locations and the final design; it has not been fully assessed. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information, access, correction, withdrawal, deletion or complaint to a privacy regulator. A verified request channel must be published before remote collection starts.

9. Children, research and automated decisions

Memora is a recreational game, not a clinical service, scientific diagnosis or formal research study. A future adaptive model may adjust game difficulty, but must not make decisions about medical care, education or other important life opportunities. Research involving minors or sensitive inferences would require additional safeguards and, where applicable, independent review.

10. Service providers and transfers

GitHub Pages provides site hosting. Supabase is the proposed backend provider for optional future training records. The project does not currently transmit gameplay records to Supabase. Technical hosting data may be processed under providers' own terms and privacy notices. Additional processors, if added, will be disclosed before the corresponding collection starts.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Memora changes. Material changes to the optional training program will be explained before collection and will require a new choice where appropriate. The effective date above identifies this version.

12. Items to finalize before activating uploads

  • Publish operator identity and a working privacy contact.
  • Confirm applicable law, youth eligibility, and any guardian or regulator requirements.
  • Specify remote retention, deletion response timelines, and cross-border processing disclosures.
  • Complete and test secure ingestion, consent verification, withdrawal and deletion.
  • Replace the existing local-only agreement with a finalized, explicit upload agreement.

Until these are completed, AI-training uploads will remain disabled.
