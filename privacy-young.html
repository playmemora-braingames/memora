🧠 Your privacy, explained simply

This page is written for younger players, especially anyone under 15. Updated November 1, 2026.

🎮 What does Memora remember?

It remembers your scores, levels and how you did in games so you can come back and play. This information stays in your browser on your device.

🔒 Does Memora send my game results away?

No, not right now. Memora is not sending your game results to an AI database. The website's hosting company may still see ordinary technical information when you visit, like other websites do.

🤖 Why does it ask about helping the AI?

After two completed levels, you might see a question about helping Memora learn how hard its games should be. This is a future idea. Even if you press “I agree,” no game results are sent right now. You can say no and still play.

🎂 What about my age?

Memora asks you to choose an age range, not your birthday. If you're under 13, you cannot join the future data-sharing program. If you're 13 or 14, your results also will not be uploaded under the current adult-only plan. Older teenagers are not included in that plan either.

🙋 Does Memora need my name?

No. You don't need to tell Memora your real name, email or birthday to play.

🗑️ Can I erase my game history?

Yes. Go to Worlds → Privacy settings → Clear local gameplay data. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings. This erases the local game history, not technical website logs or information an older version might have sent.

⭐ Remember

You can play without sharing game results for AI training. You can always choose “No thanks.” If Memora ever starts collecting data for real, it will need to explain that clearly and ask again.

If something about privacy is confusing, ask a trusted adult to read the full policy with you. Memora has not yet published a privacy contact, so remote data collection is still off.
