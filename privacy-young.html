Memora privacy, explained simply

Memora is for adults aged 18 and older.

Your progress stays in your browser. After two completed levels, you can choose whether to help improve the game. If you say yes, only future game results are uploaded to a secure database in the United States. If you say no, you can still play.

Uploaded results include your broad age range, the game played, the level, whether you answered correctly, your response time, and when you played. We don't ask for your name or birthday.

We may keep uploaded results indefinitely until you request deletion. Open Privacy settings and press “Request deletion of my uploaded data” to remove your linked records and stop future uploads. Do not clear browser storage before your deletion request succeeds.
