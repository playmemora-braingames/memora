Help improve Memora’s AI?

You have completed two levels! You can choose whether to contribute results from future games only to improve how Memora adjusts difficulty. The first two levels will never be included.

If you opt in, future game results will be securely uploaded to our US-hosted database. The data includes your age range, game type, level, correct or incorrect answers, approximate response time and difficulty settings. We do not request a name, email, birthday, location or health details. A random identifier connects your sessions and allows deletion, so records are pseudonymous.
